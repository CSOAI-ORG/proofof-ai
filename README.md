## 📏 Measurement & Statutory Mapping (Not Certification)

**Measurement, not certification.** CSOAI Ltd / Council of AI measures, signs, and publishes verifiable evidence; it does **not** certify, accredit, issue legal-compliance determinations, or act as a notified body.

Statutory risk & evidence mapping areas tracked alongside live [`GET https://councilof.ai/api/gspc`](https://councilof.ai/api/gspc) (read `totals.public_count` and `totals.lid` live — never hardcode counts):

- Article 9 — Risk Management System evidence mapping
- Article 13 — Transparency & Instructions for Use artefacts
- Article 15 — Robustness & Bias Evaluation records
- Article 26 — Deployer / FRIA evidence support (where applicable)
- Article 50 — AI Content Provenance & Watermarking records (where applicable)

Verification of supported signed records is **free forever** at [`https://councilof.ai/gspc-verify`](https://councilof.ai/gspc-verify).
